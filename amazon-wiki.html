Forward-looking Week of Cover with amazon in-network inventory relative to forecasted demand

Item unavailable for purchase due to insufficient in-network inventory, DF inventory, or no inbound inventory

Inventory exceeds healthy range vs demand, leading to increase storage costs

GMS, Ordered Revenue, Average Selling Price (ASP)

Measures top-line retail performance. GMS/Ordered Revenue = total customer purchase value. ASP = revenue ÷ units sold. These measure Amazon’s top line growth and price stability
Core Metric(s): GMS, Ordered Revenue, ASP, Units Sold
Data Availability: Available in Retail Analytics

Pure Profit Margin (PPM)

This is also called front margin. It's Amazon's margin before vendor funding. (GMS-PCOGS)/GMS
Core Metric(s): PPM
Data Availability: Not directly shown to vendors; can be calculated or obtained from VM/CSMs

Net Pure Profit Margin (NPPM,NPPU)

Amazon’s "gross" profit after vendor terms and funding. Highly influential metric vendor managers monitors. [GMS - PCOGS + Vendor Funding (including deal funding)]/GMS
Core Metric(s): NPPM,NPPU
Data Availability: Available in Retail Analytics; can also be calculated or obtained from VM/CSMs

Contribution Margin (CM,CPPU)

Amazon’s profitability after variable inbound and fulfillment costs (picking, packing, shipping, customer service, damages etc.). CM sometimes drives decisions around ordering, and deal approval.
Core Metric(s): CM,CPPU
Data Availability: Not available to vendors, but we cn model out large variable cost drivers (inbound shipment costs, outbound shipping cots etc.) and estimate CM

Contribution Margin Less Fixed Cost (CMLF,CPLF/U)

Amazon’s most complete unit economics profitability measure: CM minus Amazon’s allocated fixed costs (FC overhead, storage, etc.). CMLF determines long-term financial viability ordering
Core Metric(s): CMLF,CPLF/U
Data Availability: Not available to vendors, but we cn model out large fixed cost drivers (Storage costs) and estimate CMLF
